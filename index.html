Airline Passenger Satisfaction Analysis

Analysis of airline passenger data to understand the impact of delays and service quality on satisfaction.

View Details

Key Insights

  • Flight delays significantly reduced satisfaction across all classes.
  • Business-class passengers had higher expectations and sharper dissatisfaction.
  • WiFi quality and seat comfort were major dissatisfaction drivers.

Recommendations

  • Improve delay management and passenger communication.
  • Enhance in-flight comfort and connectivity.

Retail Sales Performance Analysis

View Details

Key Insights
