Analyzing the Operational and Cost Impact of Flight Delays on Passenger Satisfaction

Tools: Python, Power BI

This project analyzes airline passenger data to understand how flight delays and service quality affect customer satisfaction and operational performance.

Problem Context: Airlines face high operational costs and declining customer satisfaction due to frequent flight delays and service quality challenges. Understanding how different passenger segments respond to delays is essential for improving operational efficiency and customer experience.
Business Questions:
  • How do flight delays impact overall passenger satisfaction?
  • Do business-class and economy passengers respond differently to delays?
  • Which service quality factors most strongly influence dissatisfaction during delayed flights?
Approach:
  • Cleaned and prepared airline passenger data using Python.
  • Analyzed delay patterns and satisfaction scores across travel classes.
  • Evaluated service factors such as WiFi quality, seat comfort, and inflight services.
  • Visualized key trends and findings using Power BI dashboards.
Key Insights:
  • Business-class passengers reported higher satisfaction overall but showed sharper dissatisfaction when delays exceeded acceptable thresholds.
  • Flight delays had a strong negative impact on passenger satisfaction across all travel classes.
  • Poor WiFi quality and seat comfort significantly contributed to lower satisfaction scores, especially during delayed flights.
Recommendations:
  • Prioritize delay reduction strategies for high-traffic routes and peak travel periods.
  • Improve communication and compensation policies for delayed business-class passengers.
  • Invest in reliable onboard WiFi and improved seating comfort to reduce dissatisfaction during unavoidable delays.

Retail Sales Performance and Customer Insights (SQL + Power BI)

Tools: MySQL Workbench, Power BI

This project analyzes retail sales data to understand revenue drivers, customer behavior, and category performance. The goal was to generate insights that can help retail businesses improve sales strategy, customer targeting, and revenue growth.

Problem Context: Retail businesses often struggle to identify which product categories and customer segments contribute most to revenue, as well as when sales peak. Without this clarity, marketing and inventory decisions may be inefficient.
Business Questions:
  • Which product categories generate the highest sales?
  • Which customer segments contribute most to revenue?
  • Do sales perform better on weekdays or weekends?
  • How important are returning customers to total sales?
Approach:
  • Cleaned and explored retail sales data using SQL.
  • Analyzed sales trends by product category, customer demographics, and time.
  • Identified high-performing segments and repeat customer contribution.
  • Built an interactive Power BI dashboard to visualize key insights.
Key Insights:
  • Electronics and Clothing were the top revenue-generating categories.
  • Female customers aged 25–40 made the highest number of purchases.
  • Weekend sales consistently outperformed weekday sales.
  • Returning customers contributed significantly to total revenue.
Recommendations:
  • Increase inventory and promotions for high-performing categories like Electronics and Clothing.
  • Design targeted marketing campaigns for high-value customer segments.
  • Run weekend-specific promotions to maximize sales.
  • Introduce loyalty programs to encourage repeat purchases.

Workforce Planning and Layoffs Trend Analysis (SQL)

Tools: MySQL Workbench

This project analyzes global layoffs data to understand workforce reduction trends across industries, companies, and regions. The goal was to generate insights that can support strategic workforce planning, cost optimization, and risk awareness for organizations.

Problem Context: Economic uncertainty and rapid industry changes have forced many organizations to downsize. Without data-driven insights, companies risk making reactive workforce decisions that may negatively impact long-term performance and employee morale.
Business Questions:
  • Which industries experienced the highest number of layoffs?
  • Which countries were most affected by workforce reductions?
  • How did layoffs trend over time between 2020 and 2023?
  • What patterns can organizations learn to improve workforce planning?
Approach:
  • Cleaned and standardized global layoffs data using SQL.
  • Analyzed layoffs by industry, company, country, and year.
  • Identified periods and sectors with the highest workforce reductions.
  • Summarized findings to support strategic workforce insights.
Key Insights:
  • Layoffs increased significantly between 2020 and 2023.
  • The technology and startup sectors were the most affected.
  • The United States recorded the highest number of layoffs globally.
  • Layoffs often coincided with periods of economic uncertainty.
Recommendations:
  • Organizations should strengthen workforce forecasting during economic uncertainty.
  • Diversifying revenue streams may reduce the need for large-scale layoffs.
  • Proactive workforce planning can help companies manage costs while retaining key talent.
