Airline Passenger Satisfaction Analysis

Analyzed airline passenger data to understand how flight delays and service quality affect customer satisfaction.

Insights & Recommendations

Key Insights

  • Flight delays had a strong negative impact on passenger satisfaction.
  • Business-class passengers showed sharper dissatisfaction during long delays.
  • Poor Wi-Fi and seat comfort worsened dissatisfaction during delays.

Recommendations

  • Improve delay management and passenger communication.
  • Invest in reliable in-flight Wi-Fi and comfort.
  • Introduce compensation strategies for high-value passengers.
